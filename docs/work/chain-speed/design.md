# Design of topic chain-speed

> The four approved sections: what the user gets, the order, a failed final, the check.

## 1. What the user gets

The four skills lighter and faster by the seven levers of the LGS game chain (R6–R13); the stand `tests/chain` lighter so the check is cheap (R2, R3).

## 2. Order

The user closes `multi-agent` (R1) → the reduced stand (R2, R3) → the "before" series with the current skills (R4) → several iterations of skill edits by subagents, each followed by checks without a model (R5) → one final "after" series with all levers, ponytail included (R14).

## 3. A failed final

The levers are reverted one at a time, riskiest first, at most two extra series, then a stop with the numbers (R16).

## 4. Check

Success by R15; below the saving threshold — R17. Budget: two series of 2 runs on the reduced stand with the judges, about $100–110, plus at most two series of R16, each up to about $50.
