The chain `sbrain → splan → swork → saway` gets the seven levers already applied and measured on the LGS game chain (`lgs-wiki/skills/game/*` of `~/Documents/7rings/lgs-front-designer`): less text, fewer subagents, fewer re-checks. ## Task

A chain run is slow and costly for the user. The s-skills were built on the LGS skills, and on the LGS chain the same levers turned a code task from 1348 s / $7.03 into 427 s / $3.02 with the same or better code. The user asks to apply them here too, with quality and accuracy kept and without spending many tokens on the check.

## Success criteria

- R15 holds on the final series: time and tokens per run lower by at least 20 %, quality not worse.
- The cost of the check stays at two series on the reduced stand, plus at most two series of R16.
