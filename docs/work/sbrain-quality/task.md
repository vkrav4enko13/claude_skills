# Task of topic sbrain-quality

> The stand checks only how `sbrain` writes cards from a ready summary; the survey and the grill of a plan go unmeasured.

## Task

The owner runs `sbrain` for every new topic and wants it more accurate. Today the stand starts `brain` from a confirmed summary: the questions, the recommendations, the hypothesis and the grill of a plan are never run. The judge has 14 `sbrain` rules, all about the cards. A text edit of `sbrain` can make the survey worse and nothing would show it.

## Success criteria

- A stand series runs the survey and the grill of `sbrain` and gives three numbers per run: survey rules violated, persona decisions carried into the cards, planted plan flaws found — R6.
- After the text fixes the three numbers are not worse than the baseline series on current texts, and at least one is better — R9.
- The full chain series still passes the gate against `en` — R10.
