# Scope of topic sbrain-quality

> What is out of scope, what is assumed and what is still open.

## Out of scope

- The other three skills and their stand stages — they stay as they are.
- Raising the effort of any role — the chain runs strictly at `medium`.
- The paths "Probe", "Small" and "Non-software" of `sbrain` — the stand measures "Large" and "Grill".
- Real users and real projects — the stand only.

## Assumptions

- One persona and one grill plan are enough to see a difference between series; a second persona is a later topic.
- A stand-in user can answer only from the persona; a question the persona does not cover gets "I don't know, decide yourself".
- A survey series costs about as much as a brain and plan stage at `medium`, unknown until the first.

## Open questions

- none
