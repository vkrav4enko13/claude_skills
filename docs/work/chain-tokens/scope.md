# Scope of topic chain-tokens

> What is out of scope, what is assumed and what is still open.

## Out of scope

- A smaller model for any role and fast mode — ruled out by the user earlier.
- The protections kept earlier and the trap tables — they stay as they are, R29.
- The number and the set of reviewers — the check matters more than the cost.
- Running subagents at the same time — it cuts wall time, not tokens, and tokens lead.
- The cost of the `sbrain` survey itself — the stand starts `brain` from a ready summary, there is no measurement.
- Work projects and the PC — only this project's sessions and the stand were measured.

## Assumptions

- Checked by a probe on 2026-10-13: the level pinned in a skill reaches its subagents, nested ones too, but holds for one turn only — [note](../../notes/opus55-effort.md).
- The user has no observations of their own about what is superfluous: the open question went unanswered, the lever list comes from the measurements alone.
- The judge stays at `xhigh`, so that verdicts of all series are comparable.
- "Seed found" is the judge's opinion that a reviewer named the defect, not a test of the code; series `en` has all five found in all three runs.
- Whether the plan reviewer reads skill files outside the stand is unknown: on the stand the skills lie inside the project.

## Open questions

- The stand is a toy task. The gate proves quality there, not on a large real codebase: the first real tasks after the switch are to be watched by eye. Undo is one edit — the level is pinned in the skills.
- What a series at `medium` costs is unknown until the first one.
